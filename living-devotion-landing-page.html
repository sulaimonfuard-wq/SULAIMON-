What is devotion from God’s perspective?

This book explores that question and shows that true devotion is neither secondary nor inconsequential. It is active, powerful, liberating, and living.

Written to enrich and encourage readers toward a greater love of God, it looks at devotional reading and prayer while guiding you to participate personally with God and abide in Christ through “Living Devotion.”

Achievable and practical

Faith nurtured in ways that fit real, busy lives while staying biblically grounded and spiritually fulfilling.

Open to every Christian

Living Devotion is available without compulsion, with no exceptions and no sense of spiritual exclusiveness or superiority.

Gentle in tone

Guidance that is not overly theological, legalistic, demeaning, or patronizing, centred on being with God rather than doing for him.

The whole desire of God

All God has ever wanted from believers is reciprocated love, and for us to experience and enjoy his presence continually in our lives.

About the author

Robert John Spriddell is passionate about Christian devotion and has a deep personal interest in biblical theology, hermeneutics, and monastic writings. Over many years, he has taught Bible studies in group settings and at various churches as a lay visitor. He runs his own business in real estate and is a co-founder of a newly formed artistic and architectural movement. He is English and lives in Cambridge, England.
